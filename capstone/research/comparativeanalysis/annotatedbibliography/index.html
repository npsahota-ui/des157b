Annotated Bibliography

Klein, Ezra. "AI Jobs, Unemployment and Silicon Valley." The New York Times, 3 May 2026, www.nytimes.com/2026/05/03/opinion/ai-jobs-unemployment-silicon-valley.html.

This New York Times article shares a hopeful view about the future job market with the rise of AI and eases fears of unemployment and decrease in human connection. This is relevant to my capstone project because it addresses the anxieties that many people feel about their jobs and being replaced by AI, emphasizing how technological advancements have happened in the past that caused people to feel this same fear and uncertainty. It provides a positive outlook and emphasizes how people will always need and strive for human connection, no matter what. The author, Ezra Klein, was a columnist and editor at The Washington Post before joining Opinion in 2021.

"Mental Health Study Shows Gen Z Overwhelmed but Undeterred by Unrelenting Global Crises." UNICEF, 3 June 2025, www.unicef.org/partnerships/mental-health-study-shows-gen-z-overwhelmed-undeterred-unrelenting-global-crises.

This article speaks about how Gen Z is a generation that is extremely involved with social issues and has heavy media consumption, resulting in a worsening mental health state. It includes statistics from a UNICEF led partnership that showcases Gen Z’s overwhelm of current events and how the generation lacks knowledge of who to turn to when expressing mental health issues and overwhelm due to stigma. I want to use this in my capstone project because of the numbers it provides, as the age range they surveyed (14-25) is my target audience. The article is from UNICEF, an agency that works to protect and improve the lives of children.

Wang, Long, et al. "Mindfulness-Based Music Therapy for Mental Health in Senior College Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial." Frontiers in Psychology, vol. 16, October 2025, p. 1649395, https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1649395.

This study was conducted to discover whether or not mindfulness-based music therapy was helpful for easing depressive and anxious feelings in college seniors. It was found that MBMT was effective in decreasing these negative mental health symptoms while also improving sleep quality for the college students in the study. I wanted to incorporate this into my capstone project because I want to provide my audience with a different way to cope with negative feelings they may be feeling due to uncertainty of the future. The authors consist of a professor, a researcher, an assistant professor, and an intern all from universities in China.
