Usability Results

Here's what I noticed during user testing!

Add something that signal users to scroll

While observing users interacting with the site, I noticed that many of them didn’t realize they needed to scroll to see more content. This is a common issue with single-page applications where the layout doesn’t immediately indicate that there’s more information available below the fold.

Figure out a better conclusion

After seeing people navigate to the end of the page, I realized that they expected more and were unsure whether or not they had gotten through all of the content. I realized just how short my project seemed and want to add more for the user to interact with.

Fix the submit button for some questions

Users had trouble tryinhg to submit their answers for some of the text boxes and would click numerous times in hopes that something would happen. I need to fix the functionality and also want to add something that signals to users that their input has been received.
