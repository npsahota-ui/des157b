Feasability Report

A list of features I plan to include:

For my project, I want to include libraries such as animate on scroll, text animate, and the GSAP motion path plugin. I don’t have an entirely clear idea of whether or not I will be using the motion path plugin because I’m still debating whether or not I want to make my project tell a story/ a narrative. I also want to involve the user and ask questions and have them input an answer, as well as draw out any graphics that I plan to use.

The technologies required to build each feature:

As I stated previously, I would have to use different libraries and plugins to achieve what I’ve been envisioning. In terms of graphics, I wanted to hand draw them and scan them because I think it will give my project a more messy feeling, which goes with my concept. I may use Photoshop or Illustrator for some graphics or to touch up what I’ve drawn.

An assessment of whether I am skilled with each technology required, or whether I could reasonably become skilled enough within the timeframe of the project:

While I’m definitely not a pro, I think I am able to implement these technologies into my design. I have used animate on scroll before and it was relatively straightforward, so I’m sure I’ll be able to figure things out within the timeframe of this project. Text animate also seems to be relatively simple from what I’ve seen.

Difficulties I have uncovered with the development of my project that will change the project significantly since the proposal:

The main difficulty I am facing is figuring out what I want to do with my idea! I’ve been having a hard time trying to execute things piece by piece and sketching out ideas because I end up thinking that it’s straying away from what I want to convey. Sometimes it’s very hard for me to do things step by step, because I usually just jump into creative endeavors and plan along the way, rather than plan first and execute later.
