Capstone Project Proposal

The topic that I wanted to focus on for my capstone project was the uncertainty that comes with growing up and adapting to a world that is constantly changing. As a senior in college who is pursuing design in this new age of AI, it's daunting and scary to think about how my desired career path is already changing, and I've yet to even walk the stage.

For my topic, I may rely on challenging mental models and utopian narratives. I want to create something that has an all around positive message and focus on the things I can do to adapt to these changes, even though they're scary.

I was thinking of aving my project follow some kind of storyline, or have three completely different vibes for the past, present, and future. I really liked using the animate on scroll library for my last project, so I may implement it in this one as well. I think I also want to have the text be animated.

My target audience for this project is people who are just like me, about to graduate and are feeling unsure and anxious about their future and what they really want for themselves.
