Project Overview

My project is about experiencing fear of the future and is targeted towards an audience in their early twenties who are about to finish college or are in an uncertain stage of their life. I decided to focus on this topic because it is something that my peers and I often discuss, and I wanted my project to express how even though we experience fear, we are never alone.

My goal for this project was to provide a more hopeful outlook on the future and provide relief for the user if they are feeling overwhelmed by uncertainty. I included stories from peers of fears they had that they overcame to show the viewer that everyone has experienced anxieties and has still managed to work through them.

It was important for me to emphasize connection and support in my project, because it's so easy to get caught up in your worries and forget that there are people to talk to and get through things together.

Project Research

When conducting research for this project and doing a compartive analysis, I foun that I was more drawn to sites that looked more like an infographic and had more statistics and research data. Initially, I wanted my project to be less emotionally driven and more focused on providing factual information and data-driven insights. But I later realized bthat I wanted to make something that is personal and relatable, and that I wanted it to be a postive message for the future rather than creating a dystopian narrative.

I enjoyed the the greyscale color palette that some sites had as well, and did end up incorporating it into my design. However, I also wanted to include some vibrant colors to represent hope and positivity. Ultimately, I decided to use my intuition and the feedback from my peers to guide my design choices.

Visual Design

I started off with having a dark grey background with white text to give a sense of seriousness and depth, as well as an almost robotic feel to the page to emphasize the feeling of isolation and disconnection. As you scroll, the page transitions to a vibrant yellow with pink bubbles in the background to provide a much happier atmosphere.

I wanted to add photos of people and peers to emphasize the importance of connection and support and show snippets of their stories, almost like a small journal entry for each person. At the very end, there is a pixilated fire animation that allows the user to burn the worries that they typed out in the textbox above the fire. I wanted the user to see their worry disappear in a symbolic and satisfying way.

User Interface

This project allows the user to scroll the down the page as if they are walking through a story. I made it so that once you submit answers in the text boxes, the page will auto scroll to the next section as a way to naturally transition to my next point. I thought it would would be a nice way to allow te user to think about their answers and then absorb the points that I am trying to make.

In terms of cognitive load, I kept my designs throughout each section relatively simple and wanted the contrast in colors to most of the work. I felt that keeping it simple would be the most effective for my idea, because my project includes a good amount of reading. I also used only one font throughput the entire page, aside from the title, to keep up with this simplicity.

I wanted the user to be more reflective while navigating this page, so that is why I had them complete these tasks:

  1. Answer questions about their fears of the future.
  2. Answer questions about positive experiences in their life.
  3. Click on photos of people and read their stories.
  4. Type their worries and watch it dissolve in the pixelated fire.

Revisions

During user testing, I found that people had difficulties with submitting their answers to the questions, causing difficulty navigating the next sections of the site. I fixed the text boxes so that they worked properly and auto scrolled once the user sumbitted. I also added the fire section to give the project a more finsished ending.

If I had more time to work on this project, I would to add more graphics and animations to make it more visually appealing. As of right now I have a few drawings and photos that I made, but I want to add more visually interesting drawings here and there.

Summary

When working on this project, I leared just how messy the design process can be, especially when the topic is something open ended. I changed my mind many times, and ended up with something completely different than what I wanted to do in the beginning. I also learned that I am someone who like to design intuitively and make things up as I go.

I am most proud of the section that includes all my peers, because I feel that it was effective at giving a positive a hopeful message and made me feel better about my own worries while creating it.

I thought it was most challenging to settle down on an idea. I had difficulty figuring out what I really wanted, and felt like I needed to make something more data driven for it to be effective. But I am proud that I stuck to doing what felt the most true to me at the moment.
